\section*{How to use it}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Start from evidence.} Record confirmed rounds, time available, and recent closed-book performance.
  \item \textbf{Choose three repairs.} Prefer high-relevance gaps over broad coverage.
  \item \textbf{Attempt, compare, retry.} Keep the answer closed until the attempt ends; score observable behavior; schedule the next attempt.
  \item \textbf{Graduate carefully.} Two spaced successful attempts plus an unfamiliar follow-up is stronger evidence than fluent repetition.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{6. Readiness gate}
Do not graduate a critical gap on confidence alone.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Two successful closed-book attempts on different days.
  \item No critical rubric dimension remains weak.
  \item One unfamiliar follow-up or changed assumption succeeds.
  \item One later mixed-session attempt remains workable.
  \item A peer, mentor, or interviewer has challenged at least one claim when possible.
\end{itemize}
