\section*{How to use it}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Start with recent practice.} Record confirmed rounds, time available, and what you can do without notes.
  \item \textbf{Choose three gaps.} Work on the areas most likely to affect your actual loop.
  \item \textbf{Attempt, compare, retry.} Keep notes closed until the attempt ends; compare your answer with the criteria; schedule the next attempt.
  \item \textbf{Call a skill ready carefully.} Look for two successful attempts on different days and one unfamiliar follow-up.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{6. Readiness check}
Do not call a critical gap ready based on confidence alone.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Two successful closed-book attempts on different days.
  \item No critical scoring area remains weak.
  \item One unfamiliar follow-up or changed assumption goes well.
  \item One later mixed practice session remains workable.
  \item A peer, mentor, or interviewer has challenged at least one claim when possible.
\end{itemize}

\section*{11. Final week}
The final week is for steady practice on known gaps, not broad new coverage.
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\square$ Recheck each critical round with one closed-book baseline.
  \item $\square$ Retry only the most important weak areas.
  \item $\square$ Run one mixed simulation with an unfamiliar follow-up.
  \item $\square$ Practice the 90-second project opening and three decisions.
  \item $\square$ Confirm time zone, links, format, environment, and permitted tools.
  \item $\square$ Prepare concise questions about scope, success, collaboration, and the first 90 days.
  \item $\square$ Stop adding broad topics; protect sleep and response quality.
\end{itemize}
