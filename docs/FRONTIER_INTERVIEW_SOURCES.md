# Frontier interview source registry

Last verified: July 11, 2026

This registry supports the public Frontier labs Prep page. It records format-level evidence, not leaked question banks. Team-specific instructions from a recruiter override every summary here.

## Evidence policy

1. Official company process, candidate-guidance, engineering, careers, or job pages.
2. Reports that disclose a method, such as interviews with current engineers plus cross-checking against candidate data.
3. First-person reports used only for the experience they describe.
4. Search snippets and anonymous posts are discovery leads, not publishable evidence by themselves.

Do not turn one team assessment into a company-wide claim. Do not publish prompts covered by an NDA. Record the source date and keep the caveat beside the claim.
